Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Policy explains what Veritas collects and why. Contact: veritasbotsupport@gmail.com

1. Data we process

  • Discord data: server IDs, channel IDs, user IDs, usernames, and message content you send to the bot (commands, mentions, replies) so Veritas can answer.
  • Dashboard login: Discord OAuth profile (id, username, avatar, email if Discord provides it) and servers you can manage.
  • Settings: language, auto-chat, watchlist, and plan status stored for each server.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We store Stripe customer / subscription IDs linked to a Discord server. Card details stay with Stripe.

2. Why we use it

To run the bot, show the dashboard, apply Free/Premium/Ultra plans, prevent abuse, and support you by email.

3. AI providers

Message text needed for a reply may be sent to Google Gemini (or a similar AI provider) to generate answers. Do not send secrets or sensitive personal data to the bot.

4. Retention

Operational settings and plan records are kept while the bot is used on a server. Temporary caches/logs may be deleted periodically. You can ask us to delete server settings by emailing support (we may need to confirm you manage that server).

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We use processors needed to run the service (Discord, Stripe, hosting such as Render, AI API providers).

6. Your choices

Remove the bot from a server to stop new processing there. For dashboard access, log out. For payment data, use Stripe customer portal / billing tools where available.

7. Children

Veritas is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use it if you are under that age.
